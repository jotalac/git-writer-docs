# Introduction

If you've ever wanted to keep personal notes across multiple devices, you've likely had to choose between proprietary cloud apps (Notion, Evernote, Apple Notes) or paid synchronization services. In most cases, your data ends up stored and controlled by a third party.

**GitWriter** changes that. Built on [Git](https://git-scm.com), it gives you full control over where your notes live. Host them on your own server or with any Git provider such as GitHub, GitLab, or Codeberg.
